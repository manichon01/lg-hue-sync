How to use calibration

1. Test colours (easiest)

Open Devices → Calibration and pick Red, Green, Blue, White, Warm or Daylight. The lights show that colour as if the TV screen were completely that colour, using the same processing as normal sync. It works from the TV app itself; no HDMI source is needed.

Leave a colour selected, open the Tuning tab and change Saturation, Brightness, Gamma, Highlights or Direct RGB: the lights change straight away. Press Return to sync when you are done.

Fixing blues that look teal or green: select Blue, then try turning Direct RGB off (uses the Hue colour conversion) and lowering Saturation toward 1.0×. Very high saturation darkens pure blue.

2. Full-screen colour page (screen vs. lights)

The link Open TV capture patterns opens a full-screen page of one colour. While it is open, the lights show the same colour as the screen, through the normal colour processing, so you can compare screen and lights and adjust until they match. It works from any device: the TV’s own browser, a phone or a PC. Change the colour with the selector, Previous/Next or the arrow keys. Leaving the page returns the lights to normal sync.

A panel at the bottom has Direct RGB, Saturation and Brightness, applied live and saved, and shows the colour being sent to each light. It is hidden in full-screen mode. To compare without the panel, change the settings from another device while the colour stays on screen.
